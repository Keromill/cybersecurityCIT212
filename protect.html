Strong Passwords

Always use strong passwords that are long, detailed, and have special characters like symbols and numbers.

Multifactor Authentication

Always enable Multifactor Authentication on any accounts so even if your credentials are comprosied your account cannot be accessed.

Always Keep Software Up To Date

Ensure all of your applications, softwares, browser, operating system, etc. are the most up-to-date they can be, especially in terms of security features. This ensures any found vulnerabilites are patched out and you are using software less known to attackers.

Always Be Alert

Always be on the lookout for suspicious activity, whether that be things changing on your computer you don't remember altering, suspicious emails that don't seem right, or a link that seems suspicious. Always use caution when browsing the internet and stick to verified sources and information.

Utilize a VPN

When on public networks without secure connections a VPN can be a very useful tool creating an encrypted point of protection between your device and the internet. This makes your device harder to find and get access to.
